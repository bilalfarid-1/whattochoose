Pakistan IT Growth Paradox

Pakistan's ICT export remittances reached US$ 4.600B in FY2025–26 (21.4% CAGR from FY20). However, 71.8% of hiring demand in surveyed P@SHA member firms is concentrated in experienced roles, creating high qualification friction for fresh graduates.

The Verification Bottleneck

As code synthesis costs fall (\(C_{\text{gen}} \downarrow\)), the primary human bottleneck shifts to automated verification (\(C_{\text{verif}} \uparrow\)), relational schema integrity, and defensive architecture.

Optimal Strategy: FastAPI + PostgreSQL + PyTest
